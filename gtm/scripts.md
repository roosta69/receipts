# GTM Scripts — Cold Call, Instagram DM, Cold Email

Three channels run in parallel. Same pitch architecture, different medium. Every number has a source in `stats/claims-bank.md`. Pull fresh competitor data **before** every outreach attempt.

---

## Pre-call / pre-DM prep (always do this — no exceptions)

For each prospect, take 4 minutes to pull:

1. **Their current Google review count and star rating.** Google their spa name + city → click their GBP listing.
2. **Top 3 competitor review counts in their city.** Google "med spa near [city]" → record top 3 organic Local Pack listings.
3. **The gap.** "[Spa] has X reviews / Y stars. Top 3 in [city] average Z reviews / W stars."
4. **The owner's first name.** From their About page or Instagram bio. If it's not their name on the GBP listing, find it.
5. **Whether LSAs run in their metro.** Search "med spa [city]" — if you see Local Services Ads above the map pack, the CTR gap from #3 → #1 is ~7×, not 1.17×. Note this.

If you skip this prep, you sound like every other agency cold-pitching them. The competitor numbers are the entire pitch.

## Cold Call

### Best hours

Tue-Thu, **10:00–11:30 AM** and **4:00–5:30 PM** local to the prospect. Avoid Mondays (admin catch-up), Fridays (Botox parties / packed schedule), and 12-2pm lunch windows.

NP/RN-owned solo practices: the owner usually answers between appointments, especially late morning when she's resetting the treatment room.

### Script (60-90 seconds total)

> **Hi, this [Owner's first name]?**
>
> [Pause for confirmation]
>
> **My name's [Tom/Max]. I'm not selling staffing or insurance. I pulled the top 3 med spas within 5 miles of [their spa] this morning. They average [Z] Google reviews and [W] stars. You have [X] and [Y]. Reviews are now 20% of Google's local pack ranking algorithm — that's the second-biggest factor — so that gap is why [top competitor name] shows up above you.**
>
> **We close gaps like this in 90 days. We're med-spa specialists. $499 to set up, $35 per published review after that, no contract.**
>
> **Worth 30 seconds for me to send you a one-page audit of where you're losing visibility?**

### What just happened in 90 seconds

- **Identified yourself in 4 words** → not a recruiter, not insurance (the two things that get hung up on)
- **Specific competitor data** → proves you did homework, not a script
- **One hard fact with implied source** → Google's local pack 20% review weight, BrightLocal 2026
- **Named their competitor** → makes it real, not abstract
- **Productized offer with concrete numbers** → not "let's discuss your needs"
- **Tiny ask** → not "30-min demo," just "send you a one-page audit"

### If they say yes
"Great — send me your spa's full name and zip and I'll have the audit in your inbox tomorrow. Best email?"

→ Pull their email, send the audit within 24 hours, follow up in 48 if no reply.

### Common objections + responses

**"I already use [Birdeye/Podium/Growth99]."**
> "Got it. Two things — first, are you on month-to-month or annual contract? [If annual:] When does that come up? [If month-to-month:] When you compare review velocity over the last 90 days vs. your top 3 competitors, are you closing the gap or losing it? I can run that comparison and send it over either way — no obligation."

**"Send me an email."**
> "Will do. What email's best? Just so it doesn't get filtered, can I put 'audit for [their spa name]' in the subject?" → Get email, send within 24 hours.

**"How is this different from [incumbent]?"**
> "Three things. We only do med spas, so the AI replies don't accidentally name procedures and trip HIPAA. Pricing is per-review, so you only pay if it works. And no annual contract — you can leave any month. Worth me sending the audit?"

**"What's the catch?"**
> "Cashflow risk for us, not you. We do work upfront and only get paid when reviews land. We bet on our own execution. If we don't perform, we don't eat."

**"How much per review again?"**
> "$35 per published verified review. Capped at $899/month per location, so worst case you pay $899. Most spas land at $300-700/mo based on activity."

**"I'm not interested."**
> "Totally fine. Can I send the one-page audit anyway? Even if you never work with us, it'll show you exactly where the visibility gaps are. Best email?"

### Daily target

30 dials per founder per day. At 10-15% connect rate = 3-5 conversations per founder per day. At 20% conversation → audit-sent rate = 1 audit per founder per day. At ~20% audit → close = 1 customer every 2-3 weeks per founder cold-call-only.

That's slow. Run IG DM in parallel.

## Instagram DM

Med spa owners live in DMs. They check 10-30× per day. They're trained to respond (it's how they qualify prospective patients). No gatekeeper exists — you talk directly to the owner.

### Account setup

Send DMs from a personal-looking founder account, not a brand account. "Tom @ [Brand]" or just "Tom Norton" with a clean bio: "Helping med spas close the Google review gap. DM for a free audit." 1 founder photo, 3-4 posts that establish credibility (one before/after of a spa we've helped, one stat-image, one founder-story image, one CTA).

Brand accounts get auto-filtered. Personal accounts with relevant content get read.

### DM #1 — opener (within 24 hours of finding the prospect)

> Hey [first name] — saw your spa on a "best of [city]" list. Quick question: when you compare your Google reviews to [top competitor name in their city], are you tracking the gap?
>
> Pulled both this morning — they're at [Z] reviews vs. your [X]. Reviews are now 20% of Google's local pack ranking weight (BrightLocal 2026), so the gap matters more than it used to.
>
> Would a one-page audit of where you're losing visibility be useful? Free, no pitch.

### What's working in DM #1

- **Personal hook** — saw their spa, named a competitor, no template feel
- **Specific numbers** — proves homework
- **One stat** — establishes credibility
- **Tiny ask** — free audit, "no pitch"

### DM #2 — if they reply "yes" or "tell me more"

> Awesome. I'll have it over in 24 hours. Two questions to make it useful:
>
> 1. What's your spa's full name + zip? (Just to confirm I pulled the right GBP)
> 2. Which 1-2 competitors do you actually worry about? (I'll run the gap on those specifically)
>
> If you have GBP Insights handy, screenshot of monthly views/calls would tighten the math — totally optional.

### DM #2 — if they reply "what's this for"

> Building a service that closes Google review gaps for single-owner med spas. Pricing is per published review, no contract — you only pay if we perform. I'm doing free audits this month for 10 spas in [their state] to test our pull data. No pitch attached, just the report. Want yours?

### DM #2 — if no reply after 4 days

> Bumping this — totally fine if it's not a fit, no follow-up needed. Just figured the gap data was worth seeing.

### DM #3 — if no reply after another 7 days

→ Stop. Don't be that DM person.

### Daily target

50-80 DMs per founder per day. Manual, founder-led — Instagram penalizes mass-DM behavior aggressively.

Funnel math (industry benchmark): 1,000 DMs → 200 responses → 40 calls → 8-10 closes (Source: LeadResponse Instagram DM stats, ~32% personalized DM response rate vs 18% email).

## Cold Email

Lower-yield than DM but cheap to scale. Use for follow-up after a missed call, or for spas with no Instagram presence.

### Subject line

> Audit for [Spa Name]

That's it. No "quick question," no "saw your spa." Functional subject line with their name reads as a real audit, not marketing.

### Body — opener (~80 words)

> [Owner first name],
>
> Pulled your Google review profile against the top 3 med spas within 5 miles of you this morning. You're at [X] reviews / [Y] stars; they average [Z] / [W]. Reviews are 20% of Google's local pack ranking weight (BrightLocal 2026), so that gap directly affects how often you show up above them in "med spa near me" searches.
>
> I built a one-page audit showing exactly where you're losing visibility and what reaching parity would mean for monthly bookings. Want it? No pitch attached.
>
> [Tom/Max]
> [Brand] — Google reviews for med spas, paid per result.
> [phone] · [website]

### Body — follow-up #1 (5 days later, no reply)

> Bumping. Audit's still ready — just need a "yes" and your zip to confirm GBP.

### Body — follow-up #2 (10 days after #1, no reply)

> Last one — closing the file. Quick recap: [X] reviews vs. competitor average [Z]. If it's not a priority right now, no problem. Door's open whenever.

→ Stop after #2. Don't burn the email.

### Sender setup

- Use a domain that's NOT your main brand site (warmup-burnable). E.g., `audits.[brand].com` or a separate domain entirely.
- Use Smartlead, Instantly, or Lemlist for warmup + sending.
- Plain-text emails only. No images, no HTML, no signatures with logos.
- 30-50 sends per day per inbox max. 5+ inboxes for scale.

### Realistic numbers

Healthcare cold email reply rates: 2-4% (positive, not just any reply). 5,000 sends/week → ~150 replies → ~30 calls → ~5-7 closes per week if you grind.

CAC math: $30-60 per closed deal vs $150-300 for cold call. Cheaper but more brittle (deliverability degrading as Google/Microsoft tighten).

## Audit deliverable (the lead magnet)

What you actually send when they say "yes, send me the audit."

One page. PDF. Their spa name in the title. Pulled the morning of send. Contains:

1. **Their numbers:** Current Google review count, star rating, monthly review velocity (last 3 months), reply rate
2. **Top 3 local competitors:** Same metrics, named explicitly
3. **The gap:** "You're behind by [N] reviews. To match top-3 ranking benchmarks (47 reviews — BrightLocal), you need [N+more] reviews."
4. **What 90 days looks like:** "If we deliver 50 published reviews in 90 days at $35 each, you spend $1,750 + $499 setup. Lift in expected monthly new patients: [low estimate – high estimate based on local pack CTR shift]."
5. **The single CTA:** "Want to start? Reply YES and we'll send the BAA + onboarding link."

That's it. One page. Don't pad it with "About Us." The audit IS the credibility.

## Who calls/DMs which channel

Split by founder strength, not equally:

- **Tom:** Instagram DMs (he's content-native) + Cold call afternoon block (4-5:30pm local)
- **Max:** Cold call morning block (10-11:30am local) + Cold email warmup + Partner channel conversations

Don't both run all three channels — you'll bounce off each other and lose data quality. Each founder owns 2 channels deeply.

## Tracking

For every outreach attempt, log in a single Airtable / Notion / Google Sheet:

```
date, founder, channel, prospect_name, city, state, channel_handle, prospect_review_count, top3_competitor_avg, response, outcome, notes
```

After 50 attempts in any channel, run the math. Kill or double down.

Kill thresholds:
- **Cold call:** <0.4% close rate after 500 dials → kill or pause. Reallocate to DM.
- **IG DM:** <5% response rate after 500 DMs → review opener. <1% close rate after 200 calls booked → review pitch.
- **Cold email:** <2% reply rate after 1,000 sends → kill, deliverability is the issue.

## What never to do

- Pre-record the cold call opening
- Use ChatGPT to write the DM personalization (owners can smell it)
- Follow up more than 3 times across all channels before stopping
- Promise a specific review count number ("I'll get you 70 reviews") — Google's terms prohibit review schemes; promise process completion + relative position instead
- DM from a brand account with logo and 0 founder face
- Open with "How are you today?" — disqualifies you instantly as B2B sales
- Open with anything other than their name and a specific number
