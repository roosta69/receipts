## Honesty caveats — read before pitching

### 1. Google review counts are the weakest data field
Google Maps blocks programmatic scraping. Most `google_review_count` cells are either `unknown` or sourced from third-party aggregators (Birdeye, Yelp, MedSpaScout, Chamber listings) — which are usually close to but not identical to actual Google counts.

**Action:** Founders should manually verify each spa's actual Google review count before calling. ~5 seconds per spa via Google Maps.

### 2. GBP URLs mostly unknown
Google blocks bot fetches of place URLs. The website + city/state combo lets you find each spa's GBP in 1 search.

### 3. The strict 5-50 review band is narrower than expected
The truest hits in the strict band are **newer 2023-2025 openings** (Phoenix Med Spa opened Feb 2024 is the prime example — RN-led, currently low review count, perfect ICP match).

Many top-tier NP/RN Sun Belt spas are now well over 50 reviews. They're flagged in the `notes` column as "established" — pursue only if relaxing criteria.

### 4. Texas physician-ownership note
Texas requires physician ownership of medical practice. The "NP/RN-owned" entries in TX are the **brand-facing operator** who founded and runs the spa; legally most have an MD as medical director. Industry norm. Not a disqualifier for cold outreach.

## Manual verification checklist per lead (before outreach)

For each row in the CSV, before calling/DMing:

- [ ] Open the website. Confirm owner credential (NP/RN/PA, not MD-only).
- [ ] Confirm single location (no "Locations" page with multiple cities).
- [ ] Look up GBP on Google Maps. Pull actual current review count + star rating. Update CSV.
- [ ] Pull top 3 nearest competitor med spas in same city. Record their review counts (this is the pitch).
- [ ] Check if Local Services Ads run in the metro. Affects CTR math in pitch.
- [ ] Find owner's first name from About page or Instagram bio. Update CSV.

This pre-call prep is ~4 min per lead. Don't skip it — the personalized competitor numbers are the entire pitch.
