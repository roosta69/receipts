## 2. The gap

**Section header:** Where you actually stand

**Body:**
> Reviews are the second-largest factor in Google's local pack ranking — 20% of the algorithm. (BrightLocal, 2026.)
>
> Top-3-ranked local businesses average 47 Google reviews. The bottom three average 38. The math is uncomplicated: your competitors' review counts decide where you appear in "med spa near me" searches.
>
> 47% of consumers won't use a business with fewer than 20 reviews. 31% require 4.5+ stars. 74% only care about reviews from the past three months. (BrightLocal Local Consumer Review Survey, 2026.)
>
> Most med spas we audit are 30 to 80 reviews behind their nearest three competitors. That gap costs them ranking, ranking costs them clicks, clicks cost them bookings.

**Mock callout (use specific city + numbers):**

> *Sample audit — Phoenix, AZ*
>
> | Spa | Reviews | Stars | Last 30 days |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Top competitor | 84 | 4.8 | +9 |
> | Second | 71 | 4.7 | +6 |
> | Third | 52 | 4.9 | +4 |
> | **You** | **22** | **4.6** | **+1** |
>
> *Gap to top 3 average (69): –47 reviews. At local-pack-#1 click rate of 17.6% vs your current #4-5 rank, that's roughly 38 fewer monthly inquiries.*

## 4. What we do

**Section header:** Three things

**Body:**
> **One.** We pull your competitive position. Top 5 nearest med spas. Review counts, ratings, recency, reply rate. Refreshed monthly.
>
> **Two.** We send review requests to every patient post-visit. Sentiment-neutral language. Sent through your existing CRM under a signed BAA. We do not gate, filter, or steer review sentiment — that's a Federal Trade Commission violation.
>
> **Three.** We reply to every review on your behalf. HIPAA-safe templates that never disclose patient or procedure information. 1-3 star reviews get human review before any reply is published.

## 6. Compliance

>

> Most "review services" don't survive a 30-second look at the Federal Register. Ours does.

## 7. The math

**Section header:** One worked example

**Body:**
> A single-location NP-owned med spa in Scottsdale. 22 Google reviews to start. 30 patient visits per week. Average $650 ticket. Five-year Botox-patient LTV: $9,000 (Dean Garland Insights).
>
> After 90 days: 38 published reviews. Local-pack ranking moves from #4 to #2. Monthly GBP views (1,260 baseline per BrightLocal) convert at a higher rate; estimated 5 incremental new patients per month.
>
> Five new Botox patients per month at $9,000 LTV = $540,000 in five-year booked revenue.
>
> Cost over those 90 days: $499 setup + ~$1,330 in review fees = $1,829.
>
> The numbers above are a model, not a promise. Your spa's GBP traffic, conversion rate, and patient mix are different. The audit we send before you sign tells you what your actual math looks like.

- **No case studies** — until we have permission to publish
- **No blog** — until customer #20 minimum

- **No icons** — anywhere

## Color and typography (design tokens)

Single page. Restrained.

```
--bg: #faf8f3            /* warm off-white */
--text: #161616          /* near-black */
--muted: #6b6b6b         /* secondary text */
--accent: #8b1a1a        /* deep burgundy — used only for the gap stat in hero */
--rule: #d4d2cc          /* hairline rules */

--font-serif: "GT Sectra", "Tiempos", Georgia, serif    /* headlines */
--font-sans: "Inter", -apple-system, sans-serif         /* body */
--font-mono: "JetBrains Mono", monospace                /* numbers, tabular */
```

Headlines use serif. Numbers (review counts, prices) use mono with tabular figures. Body in sans.

No gradients. No drop shadows. One accent color, used for one thing (the hero gap number).
